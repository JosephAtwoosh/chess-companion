Devices

Every phone, tablet or computer that has typed the family password. Ban refuses that device even if someone types the password on it again. Clearing the browser's data gets round a device ban, so for a determined visitor use Ban + address as well.

Banned addresses

A banned address can't sign in or play at all. Home and mobile addresses change from time to time, and a school or mobile network can share one address between many people, so ban an address only when you need to. A range such as 81.2.3.0/24 covers 81.2.3.0 to 81.2.3.255.
